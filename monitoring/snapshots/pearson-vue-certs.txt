Claude Certification Program by Anthropic
The Claude Certification Program validates the skills behind advising, designing, and building Claude solutions. Each certification maps to real work, with three roles to choose from: Practitioner, Architect, and Developer. Certification is open to organizations in the Claude Partner Network and counts toward partner program standing.
Certifications
Claude Certified Associate - Foundations (CCAO-F)
Claude Certified Architect - Foundations (CCAR-F)
Claude Certified Architect - Professional (CCAR-P)
Claude Certified Developer - Foundations (CCDV-F)
Digital Badges
Anthropic has partnered with Credly to provide digital certification badges.

Frequently Asked Questions
Find answers to common questions about your Pearson profile, name corrections, exam-day identification requirements, scheduling and rescheduling, delivery options, and retake policies.
How do I request a name correction?
Please submit your name correction request at least 24 hours before your scheduled exam. If you submit a name update/change request less than 24 hours before your exam, Anthropic may not be able to apply it in time. Advance notice ensures they have time to review and apply the correction before your appointment.
To request a name correction:
Email certifications-support@anthropic.com from the email address your exam is registered with, with the subject line "Name Correction Request"
Provide your First and Last name exactly as it appears on your ID — clearly label which is which
Use Latin characters only
Does the address and phone number in my Pearson profile need to match my own?
No. Your address and phone number are not used for ID verification and don't need to match your personal information.
The phone number on your record is a standard Anthropic corporate contact number, added to all Anthropic candidate profiles. It's expected to be there — no action needed. It's never used to contact you or verify your identity.
On exam day, identity is verified by matching the name on your record to your government-issued ID. The phone number and address have no effect on this process.
Anthropic cannot update address or phone number fields on candidate profiles in Pearson at this time.
What happens if my name doesn't match my ID at check-in?
Before your exam, you're required to confirm that your Pearson profile name matches your government-issued ID exactly — this requirement appears during scheduling, is included in your confirmation email, and is detailed on Pearson's Proof of Identification Requirements page.
If you notice a mismatch, request the correction from Anthropic Certification Support at certifications-support@anthropic.com before your scheduled exam.
If you arrive at your exam with a name mismatch Pearson will not allow you to test — and per policy, your exam fee is forfeited. A correction request submitted only after a denied-entry or no-show incident cannot be used to reverse that forfeiture; you'll need to pay to reschedule.
Can I reschedule or cancel?
Yes. You must contact Pearson or access your online Pearson account to cancel or reschedule your exam at least a minimum of 48 hours prior to your appointment. Exams cannot be canceled or rescheduled less than 48 hours prior to your appointment. Failure to cancel or reschedule in time or failure to appear for your appointment will result in the forfeiture of your exam fee.
When rescheduling your exam, be sure you receive an email confirming your new exam date. If you don't receive that confirmation, log back into your Pearson account to verify the new date appears in your profile — otherwise, your exam rescheduling may not have completed successfully and you risk forfeiting your exam authorization.
If I don't pass, when can I retake?
Waiting periods increase with each failed attempt: 14 days after the first, 30 days after the second, and 90 days after the third. You can take an exam up to 4 times per rolling 12 months. This applies per exam, so failing one track doesn't block you from registering for another.
I need to change my OnVUE appointment to a Test Center appointment, or vice versa. How do I do that?
To change your delivery mode, log into your
Anthropic Pearson account
and cancel your existing exam appointment. You can then reschedule your exam and select your desired delivery mode — online proctored (OnVUE) or a Pearson test center.
The standard cancellation and rescheduling policy still applies — you must cancel at least
48 hours
before your scheduled appointment, or your exam fee will be forfeited.
